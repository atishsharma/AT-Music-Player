Desktop widget

Minimize AT Music and the widget appears above your desktop. Pin keeps it on top of every window. Hover reveals pin, open and hide. Double-click opens the app.

Behaviour & motion

  • Shows on minimize. Hides when the main window is restored. Never steals focus.
  • Always on top. Pin toggles it; the choice persists. Uses the floating level, so it stays below full-screen apps.
  • Remembers position. Reopens where you dropped it, if that display is still connected.
  • Theme. Follows the app: light glass in Light, smoked glass in Dark, OLED and Glass.
  • Cheap to run. State arrives once per second; the bar interpolates locally. The orb spin pauses with playback.
