## AT Music Pro 2.0.0

The biggest update so far: eight new features, ambient mode, a new Settings page and a full bug-fix pass across the app.

### New
- **Smart Radio**: start a station from any song (right-click → *Start Radio*, or the radio button in the player bar). Similar songs keep coming; the queue refills itself.
- **Floating video & subtitles**: pop the music video out into a small always-on-top window and keep working. Close it and the song carries on from the same spot. Also adds theater mode, subtitles (uploaded or auto-generated) and a remembered video quality.
- **Phone remote**: Settings → *Phone remote* → scan the QR code with a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Play, pause, skip, seek, volume, the queue and search, all from the phone's browser. No app needed; a secret link keeps other devices out, and *Reset link* locks out old ones.
- **Crossfade & even volume**: Settings → *Playback*. Crossfade from 0 to 12 s between songs, gapless playback and automatic volume levelling so loud and quiet songs sit at a similar level.
- **Sing-along**: the microphone button in the player bar turns the lead vocals down; *Karaoke view* shows big, word-by-word lyrics.
- **Stats & yearly recap**: a new *Stats* page with time listened, top songs, artists and albums, listening streaks, time of day and a year of activity. *Recap* makes a shareable image of your year.
- **Smart playlists**: playlists built from rules (artist, length, play count, date added, last played…) that update themselves. Includes *Most played*, *On repeat*, *Recently added*, *Forgotten favourites* and *Never played*.
- **Discord status & Last.fm scrobbling**: show what you're playing on your Discord profile, and scrobble to Last.fm. Both are set up in Settings → *Connections*.
- **Ambient mode**: a full-screen screensaver with synced lyrics and calm animated scenes (Aurora, Vinyl, Horizon, Clock). It can start by itself when the computer is idle, and has a sleep timer.
- **New Settings page**: simpler, grouped and searchable (Ctrl+F).

### Improved
- System media controls now show the song's artwork, artist and album.
- The desktop widget stays on top on Linux (Wayland/X11).
- Mini player: rounded corners and a minimize button; the title bar and volume pill are gone.
- Equalizer and History are readable in every theme; a theme switch sits above Settings in the sidebar.
- Artist and album pages keep the photo or cover you chose.

### Fixed
- "Play all" and clicking a song in an album, artist, playlist or library list now play in order. Before, Next could replay the first song or jump back.
- Favourite songs play when clicked.
- YouTube songs can be added to playlists.
- Video mode no longer plays the song's audio underneath the video.
- Songs from History and Stats replay after the audio cache is cleared.
- Home → *Recommended* songs play.
- Picking a new song while another is still downloading no longer starts the old one.
- Rescanning a folder removes songs whose files were deleted.
- Lyrics with repeated lines (several timestamps on one line) show every repeat.
- YouTube titles no longer show codes like `&#39;`.
- Plus many smaller fixes to search, playlists, the Last.fm page, Fun Zone and styling.
- Linux `.tar.gz` build launches correctly.

### Notes
- Builds are not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen: *More info → Run anyway*. macOS: right-click the app → *Open* the first time (or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AT Music Pro.app"`).
- Windows and macOS builds bundle yt-dlp and ffmpeg. Linux builds bundle yt-dlp; install `ffmpeg` from your distro for video mode.
- **Phone remote**: your firewall may ask to allow incoming connections the first time; allow it on private networks.
- **Discord status** needs a Discord application ID (free at discord.com/developers). **Last.fm scrobbling** needs your Last.fm API key and shared secret (last.fm/api/account/create).
- Upgrading keeps your library, playlists, history and settings.
